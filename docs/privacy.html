VidPush

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VidPush (“the App”) handles information when you use it to sign in with Google, connect YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, or Instagram accounts, and upload videos.

1. What VidPush does

VidPush can run locally or in a private, owner-operated Modal deployment. It uses Google to verify the authorized owner's identity and separate OAuth connections to publish videos to selected platforms. Credentials are stored in the application's SQLite database: on the local computer for local use, or in the deployment's persistent storage for hosted use.

2. Information we process

  • Google identity: verified email and Google subject identifier are stored with a hash of the session token and its expiry. Login requests basic identity scopes; publishing permissions are requested separately.
  • Publishing credentials from Google/YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram, plus selected destination IDs and names, stored to support connected accounts.
  • Video files you select, temporarily processed for upload to the platforms you enable.
  • Basic metadata you provide (such as title) sent to the platforms you select.

3. Local and hosted processing

  • Local deployments process files on the local computer. In a Modal deployment, the server receives your selected video, processes the upload, and stores connection and session data in its configured storage.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. Third-party services

When you sign in, connect accounts, or upload, Google/YouTube, TikTok, and Meta receive the data needed for those operations according to their own privacy policies. Review those policies on their websites.

5. Data retention

Publishing credentials remain in the configured database until you disconnect accounts or delete application data. Facebook disconnection also removes stored Page choices. Sessions expire after seven days and are revoked on sign-out; expired session rows are removed when new sessions are created. Temporary upload files are removed after jobs complete; interrupted jobs may leave temporary files until the runtime is cleaned up or replaced.

6. Security

You are responsible for protecting your computer, `.env` secrets, and OAuth credentials. Do not share your client secrets or commit them to public repositories.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will reflect changes. If you deploy VidPush in a different environment, update this description to reflect that deployment.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the VidPush project maintainer via the contact method on the project repository.
